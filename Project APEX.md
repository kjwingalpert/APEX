# Applied Policy & Evidence Exchange (APEX)

APEX is an AI-powered B2B/B2G SaaS web application that aggregates open-access research, court decisions, and government filings into a single interactive platform, enabling non-profits and policy teams to track legislative stances, analyze historical precedent, and drive evidence-based advocacy.

The three major questions that policy researchers must answer to ensure a policy is successful:

1. **Is it correct?** Policies should be based on the best available scientific evidence and be ethical and equitable for the people closest to the problem.  
2. **Is it administratively feasible?** It is not enough for a policy to be scientifically sound; it must also be implementable, with sufficient budget, staff, resources, and skills.  
3. **Will it get support?** Policy researchers must secure the backing of both decision-makers and the stakeholders who will be directly affected by the policy outcomes. 

**What It Is:** APEX is an interactive B2B/B2G web platform engineered to help non-profits, advocacy groups, and small organizations navigate the complex landscape of government action and research. Built on top of pre-loaded open-access academic repositories, legal databases, and government APIs, the application provides AI-powered tools to scan, summarize, and contextualize policy research. It equips teams to easily track legislative evolution, evaluate policymaker stances, and transform dense data into actionable insights for high-impact strategy. 

**Mission:** Our mission is to reduce the time and resource burden on non-profits, advocacy groups, and policy researchers by delivering high-impact synthesis, verified context, and targeted legislative tracking. By lowering barriers to authoritative data, APEX empowers smaller organizations to level the playing field—helping them effectively educate decision-makers, execute direct lobbying, and build strategic coalitions backed by bulletproof evidence.

* **Internal Mission**: Eliminate academic and legal misinformation in policy advocacy by guaranteeing absolute source transparency with verifiable, sentence-level citations.

**Why?** A critical information gap exists between high-level research and its real-world application, disproportionately affecting small organizations and non-profits with limited staff and resources:

* Resource and Bandwidth Constraints: Non-profits and small teams need to educate lawmakers, deliver expert testimony, and submit regulatory comments, but lack the bandwidth to manually sift through thousands of pages of academic papers, agency filings, and historical precedent.

* Flawed Workarounds & Misinformation: Many small teams currently rely on generic AI models (like standard ChatGPT or Claude) to quickly gather context. However, these platforms are not pre-loaded with comprehensive policy databases and frequently hallucinate or generate unverified citations, putting an organization’s credibility at risk.

**Core Features:** 

* **Repository and Synthesis of Research and Policy Documents**  
  * Core Scope: Congressional, legislative, judicial, and agency research papers.  
  * Description: Functions as a centralized, AI-powered database that scans, categorizes, and summarizes complex academic, legislative, and agency documents into quick, high-impact takeaways backed by verifiable, sentence-level citations.    
* **Legislative Stances and Support Tracker**  
  * **Core Scope:** Historical actions, entity stances, and advocacy strategies (educating lawmakers, direct lobbying, and coalition building) for non-profits and small organizations.  
  * **Description:** Analyzes public statements, legislative actions, and historical arguments to provide a visual "Pro/Con" dashboard that quantifies where specific government entities and policymakers stand on an issue. This helps non-profits and small organizations strategically target direct lobbying, provide expert testimony, share high-impact community data, and form powerful coalitions to expand their collective voice.    
* **Decision and Action Tracer**  
  * Core Scope: Judicial decisions, court precedent, and regulatory timelines.  
  * Description: Leverages authoritative legal and legislative data pipelines to map out the historical precedent of an issue, allowing users to track the evolution of policies, court decisions, and regulatory actions over time.
